- Recorder: `/psrec sim on` adds sim telemetry for leveling simulators, off
  by default: out-of-combat health and mana regeneration with the Spirit and
  Intellect behind it, meals with health and mana when you sit and stand, one
  record per fight (length, health and power at the start, lowest and end,
  kills, XP, deaths, your casts, the target) and the damage one mob's swings
  did. Only your own character's values and the creatures you fight.
  `/psrec sim` shows which of these the game keeps secret right now.
